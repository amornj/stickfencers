WASD move · Left Click light chain (3) · E heavy · Q + WASD = dash in any direction (diagonals too); Q alone = dash attack · F block / parry (0.2s window)
T toggles target lock / free camera (mouse or ←/→ to turn). Ctrl locks / releases the mouse; Esc also releases it.
R = BURST after 2 seconds of stun/down time — heals 10, invincible, unique per weapon. A ready burst triggers automatically on lethal damage: land a hit within 2.6 seconds to revive with 30 health and a 5-second forcefield, or explode! Side-dash (Q+A/D) while stunned to escape early. V = quick attack (palm, kick or punch), Shift + WASD = fast dash in any direction (30 stamina; forward with no direction), Space = jump, then Left Click in the air = dropkick. Kick the crates along the walls to throw them!
Break their posture (100) to stun them, then Left Click or E for the Posture Break chain. Lethal hits trigger weapon finishers unless a ready burst saves the target.
Jetpack & Sword: RMB hold grapple · WASD steer · Space boost · airborne LMB dropkick / E sword · B replace blades · hold G grounded refuel.
Keyboard & mouse recommended. Click the game once if keys don’t respond.
